FAQ

Questions manufacturers ask us.

Something else on your mind? Ask us directly.

What is Avyntro?

Avyntro is an ERP and HRMS suite for manufacturing businesses in India. GST invoicing, GST compliance, bank reconciliation, double-entry accounting, financial reports and HR all run on one ledger, under one login.

Does Avyntro handle GST and e-invoicing?

Yes. Avyntro raises GST tax invoices, splits tax into CGST and SGST or IGST based on place of supply, creates the e-invoice IRN where your turnover requires it, builds GSTR-1 data from your invoices and shows your GST liability with due dates. You can manage several GSTINs in one workspace.

Can I try Avyntro before signing up?

Yes. The live demo runs a sample engineering business with six months of data. It needs no sign-up, and anything you change is saved only in your own browser. The demo is not connected to the real product, so it doesn't send IRNs to the GST portal or send emails.

Can I import my existing product catalogue?

Yes. Upload your catalogue as a PDF or Word file and Avyntro reads the products out of it. You check and correct them in a review step before they go into your item master.

Does Avyntro do bank reconciliation?

Yes. Import your bank statement and Avyntro matches each line to invoices, payments and expenses already in your books. You review the matches and approve the reconciliation session.

Is Avyntro available now?

Avyntro is in early access. We're onboarding manufacturing businesses in small batches. Request early access and we'll get in touch to schedule a walkthrough.

What's coming next?

CRM pipeline, NexSupport (helpdesk) and Projects are on the way, along with multi-currency, recurring billing, fixed assets, budgets, cost centres and barcode scanning. See the roadmap.
