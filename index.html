// Why one suite

Your books, your floor and your people, finally reading from the same page.

Most shops run GST invoicing in one tool, inventory on a whiteboard or a spreadsheet, and payroll somewhere else entirely — then spend the end of every month reconciling all three by hand. Avyntro keeps them on one ledger from the start.

Every transaction posts once

An invoice, a goods receipt, a payroll run — each one lands in the general ledger automatically, so your P&L, GSTR filings and bank reconciliation are never arguing with each other.

Built for Indian compliance, not bolted on

GSTR-1/3B prep, e-invoice IRN generation, TDS on payroll, and an error scanner that flags compliance and data-quality issues before you file — not after a notice arrives.
