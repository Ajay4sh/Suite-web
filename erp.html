ERP — operations & accounting, on one ledger.

GST invoicing, inventory, procurement, financial reports and compliance, all posting to the same general ledger. Built so the person closing your books and the person running your shop floor are looking at the same numbers.

GST tax invoicing

Raise GST-compliant invoices with client-specific price lists, record partial payments against an invoice, set up recurring or subscription billing, and send invoices straight over email or WhatsApp. E-invoice IRN generation is built in for the thresholds that require it.

Client price listsPartial paymentsRecurring billingEmail & WhatsApp sendE-invoice IRN

Inventory & light manufacturing

An item master and multi-warehouse stock register track what you have and where. Goods receipt notes (GRN) and delivery notes record what moves in and out, while bills of material (BOM) and production orders handle simple assembly and light manufacturing runs.

Item masterWarehousesStock registerGRNDelivery notesBOMProduction orders

Procurement

Keep a vendor list, raise purchase requests, send out RFQs and compare quotes side by side, then convert the winning quote into a purchase order. Vendor payments are tracked through to settlement.

VendorsPurchase requestsRFQ & quote comparisonPOsVendor payments

GST filing

GSTR-1 and GSTR-3B prep pulls straight from your recorded transactions. Notices are tracked in one place, and a Tally import path brings your historical ledger and GST data across instead of starting from zero.

GSTR-1 / 3B prepNoticesTally import

General ledger & financial reports

Every module posts to the same double-entry journal and general ledger. Pull a P&L, Balance Sheet, Cash Flow statement or Trial Balance at any time, and compare budget against actual by cost centre.

Double-entry journalsP&LBalance SheetCash FlowTrial BalanceBudget vs actualCost-centre analytics

Fixed assets

Maintain a fixed asset register with depreciation schedules and disposals — each one posting to the ledger automatically, so your balance sheet never drifts from what's actually in your register.

Asset registerDepreciation schedulesDisposals

Bank reconciliation

Match your bank statements against recorded transactions to catch what's missing, duplicated, or simply hasn't cleared yet — before it becomes a month-end surprise.

Statement matching

Error scanner

Before you file, the error scanner flags compliance and data-quality issues across your invoices, ledger entries and GST data — so problems surface while they're still easy to fix, not after a notice arrives.

Compliance checksData-quality checks
