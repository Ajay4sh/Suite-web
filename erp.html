ERP — operations & accounting, on one ledger.

GST invoicing, your item catalogue, GST compliance, bank reconciliation and financial reports, all posting to one double-entry ledger. The person closing your books and the person raising invoices see the same numbers.

What ERP does today

Everything below is live in Avyntro now. Want to see it? Open the interactive demo with sample data, no sign-up needed.

GST invoicing

Raise GST-compliant tax invoices, generate a clean PDF, create the e-invoice IRN where your turnover requires it, and email the invoice to your client straight from Avyntro.

GST tax invoicesPDF generationE-invoice IRNEmail dispatch

Product & item master

Save each product or service once, with its HSN/SAC code, GST rate and price. While you raise an invoice, items autocomplete, so nobody retypes rates or tax codes.

Item masterHSN / SACInvoice autocomplete

Catalogue import

Upload your existing catalogue as a PDF or Word file. Avyntro reads the products out of it and shows them in a review step, so you correct anything before it goes into your item master.

PDF & Word uploadBulk importReview before saving

GST compliance

GSTR-1 data built from the invoices you've already raised, a liability dashboard showing what you owe, filing notices tracked in one place, and management of every GSTIN your business files under.

GSTR-1 dataLiability dashboardFiling noticesMultiple GSTINs

Clients & interaction history

Keep every client's details in one place with a running history of calls, meetings and notes, next to the invoices you've raised for them.

Client recordsInteraction history

Bank reconciliation

Upload a bank statement and Avyntro matches each line against your recorded receipts and payments. You review the suggested matches, then approve the reconciliation session.

Statement uploadAuto-matchingSession approval

Journal entries

A true double-entry ledger: every entry must balance, the trial balance is always available, and a mistaken entry is corrected with a reversal instead of being edited away.

Double-entryTrial balanceReversals

Financial reports

Profit & Loss, Balance Sheet, Cash Flow and Trial Balance come straight from the ledger, for any period, whenever you need them.
