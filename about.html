About

Why Avyntro exists.

A manufacturing business runs on three things at once — its books, its stock, and its people. We got tired of watching those three live in three different tools.

What we believe

Most manufacturing businesses end up running GST invoicing in one tool, inventory on a whiteboard or a spreadsheet, and payroll somewhere else entirely — then spend the end of every month reconciling all three by hand. That reconciliation work is overhead, not value. We think the operations side of a manufacturing business — billing, stock, procurement, people, compliance — should sit on one connected ledger, so the numbers agree with each other by construction, not by effort.

What we're building toward

ERP and HRMS are the foundation: accounting, inventory, procurement and GST compliance on one side, people and payroll on the other, both posting to the same books. CRM, NexSupport and Projects are next — built on that same foundation, so a customer record, a support ticket or a project cost is never a separate system you have to keep in sync by hand.

Where we're starting. Avyntro is built with India's GST, e-invoicing and TDS requirements as first-class features, not a bolt-on — because that's the compliance environment the product was designed against from day one. The underlying ledger and modules aren't India-specific, so the same foundation carries over as the product grows into other markets.
